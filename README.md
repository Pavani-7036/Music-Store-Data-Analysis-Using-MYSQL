# 🎵 Music Store Data Analysis | MySQL

### SQL-Based Sales Performance and Customer Behavior Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive analysis of a digital music store's relational database using **MySQL**. The analysis focuses on understanding customer purchasing behavior, evaluating sales performance across geographical regions, identifying popular music genres and artists, and uncovering opportunities for business growth.

By applying SQL techniques to 11 interconnected tables, the project transforms transactional data into meaningful insights that support data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Business Problem

The music store faces challenges in evaluating overall sales performance and understanding customer preferences because relevant information is distributed across multiple tables.

This project addresses these challenges by answering key business questions related to revenue distribution, customer spending, music preferences, and artist popularity.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technical Skills

**Database Management:** MySQL

**Development Environment:** MySQL Workbench

**SQL Techniques:**
- Joins and Aggregate Functions
- GROUP BY, HAVING, and Subqueries
- Common Table Expressions (CTEs)
- Window Functions
- Data Filtering and Sorting

## 🗂️ Database Overview

The relational database consists of 11 tables covering customer information, transactions, music collections, and employee details.

**Key Tables:** `Customer`, `Invoice`, `InvoiceLine`, `Track`, `Album`, `Artist`, `Genre`, `Employee`, `MediaType`, `Playlist`, and `PlaylistTrack`.

Relationships between these tables enable analysis of customer transactions, artist performance, and music preferences across different markets.

## 📊 Key Findings

**1. Geographic Sales Performance**

The USA recorded the highest number of invoices, with **131 transactions**, while Prague emerged as the highest-revenue city, generating **273.24 in total invoice sales**.

**2. Artist and Genre Analysis**

AC/DC had the highest number of Rock tracks, with **18 tracks**, followed by Aerosmith with **15 tracks**. Rock also emerged as a leading genre across several international markets.

**3. Customer Spending Behavior**

The analysis identified the highest-spending customers in individual countries, providing useful insights for customer segmentation and personalized marketing opportunities.

**4. Music Collection Analysis**

The project examined track durations and artist contributions to better understand the store's music catalog.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Develop targeted marketing campaigns based on country-specific music preferences.
- Introduce customer loyalty programs for high-spending customers.
- Prioritize promotional activities in high-revenue markets.
- Optimize music collections using insights into popular genres and artists.
